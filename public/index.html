How this works. Add clear photos of your factory sheet (the paper with your numbers). This app reads the numbers for you. You can correct any mistakes, then save everything as an Excel file you can open on any computer.
Step 1
Take a steady, well-lit photo of the full sheet
Step 2
Upload here, then tap “Read numbers from photos”
Step 3
Open each photo and fix any wrong numbers in the table
Step 4
Download your Excel file (one file per photo)
Add your photos
You can add up to 5 photos at a time. Use JPG, PNG, or WEBP. Each photo will get its own Excel file when you download.
